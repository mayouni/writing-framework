# Decisions of the writing framework

| Id | Date | Decision | Reason | Open |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AWF-01 | 2026-10-11 | The framework is built from the Ring book study, not from the two 2026 books' style guides | The Ring book is the only book the author wrote alone; the 2026 guides describe agents' choices (Refine's em dash, the "documentary" register) as if they were his | The author confirms which Refine/Softanza choices he owns |
| AWF-02 | 2026-10-11 | Twelve commitments, named after the twelve habits of the study (H1–H12), each with the agents' part, the author's part and a check | A rule without a check is a wish; a check without the author's part would let agents write the whole book | — |
| AWF-03 | 2026-10-11 | Exclamations are not rationed per chapter (Softanza's "one per chapter at most") but placed: only after a run or a reveal, about two per thousand words | The copyedit kept and even added them where they followed a run; the study found none of his on an abstract sentence | The Softanza brief's rule to be amended by the author |
| AWF-04 | 2026-10-11 | The colon is the book's mark; the em dash is rare | 15 colons against 0.5 dashes per 1,000 words in the Ring book; the dash was the Refine agent's habit | — |
| AWF-05 | 2026-10-11 | The noise list is enforced by a tool (`tools/style_lint.py`), the author's calls are never decided by a tool or an agent | The editor's cuts split cleanly into noise and voice; the voice items are his to rule | The author rules on the calls list (FRAMEWORK §4) |
| AWF-06 | 2026-10-11 | A hole where a particular belongs is an `[AUTHOR: …]` marker, filled by the author or, on his word, by the answer policy that invents nothing | Softanza phase 9 showed the policy works at scale (77 answers, none invented) and leaves the warmth missing, which is the honest state | — |
| AWF-07 | 2026-10-11 | Fresh-reader verification is a standing gate, not a one-time phase | Softanza phases 10–11 found 624 statements to narrow in 41 drafted pages; the overclaim is the author's reach without his concession, and agents inherit the reach | — |
| AWF-08 | 2026-10-14 | **Never undersell the work.** Limits are told as stage and direction ("in development", "to come", "the field leads here today"), not as weakness or apology; an agent never writes that nothing is in production, or that a part is "weak" or "falls short", unless the record says it; "I looked and found nothing" stays for the *absent* stage word. C3's "the loss beside the win" holds in this register | The author's standing ruling (Softanza VOICE-04): over-correction toward transparency misleads professional readers and hurts the work; agents inherit the reach and also the apologies | Settled 2026-10-14 on the author's delegation (Softanza STAGE-01): the stage words stay built / designed / absent; "in production" and "in development" are prose, used where the record shows them |
| AWF-09 | 2026-10-14 | **Type size follows semantic level** in every reader, page or print the books make: book > part > chapter > section > body text; a heading is larger than the text it heads; no label or note under 12px; one scale in the build, with a check that fails the build when the order breaks. A component never takes a size of its own | The author's standing rule, repeated across projects (Zui constitution, Softanza TYPE-01): a part title showed smaller than the chapter titles under it | None |
| AWF-10 | 2026-10-14 | **"AI model" and "AI agent" where the bare "model" or "agent" is ambiguous** (a data model, a domain model; a person's agent): at first use in a page and wherever another sense is near, never in every sentence; a book's own exact terms keep their names | The author's standing ruling (Softanza VOICE-05): the bare words are imprecise for readers outside the field | None |
| AWF-11 | 2026-10-15 | **Every book session reports to the author in the memo format of `templates/memo.md`** (by, subject, why, did, state, waiting, next, note; the date and time read from the clock), in every substantive message and not only at a phase's end | The author's ruling, recorded in the house as HOUSE-MEMO-01: "Yes: every book session, present and future, reports to me in the memo format of the writing framework's template: by, subject, why, did, state, waiting, next, note." A session follows only what it loads, so the line is in this folder's `CLAUDE.md` and in the house's | None |
| AWF-12 | 2026-10-15 | **Code in a book follows one order of forms**: the scene form with braces is the basis (three or more calls on one object); the chain for one sentence; the classic form shown; Naturally(...) shown where it runs; `@@()` stays until Haro prints the expressive form by default, and then the expressive printout is the norm in books, docs and their sites | The author's ruling, recorded in the house as HOUSE-CODE-01: "embrace the beauty of the declarative style with braces [...] this should be the basis. Of course, sometimes one sentence with chaining is a better choice and the book must make good use of both. The more classic style should also be shown [...] as well as the more sophisticated natural form using Naturally(...)". And: "@@() could disappear in Haro and let the expressive printout be the norm [...] all the help and docs in the web sites and books and their sites." (relayed by the Softanza book session, confirmed by him in the house session) | The examples of a book teach the style before the prose does | The language's own session reports when Haro prints the expressive form by default |
